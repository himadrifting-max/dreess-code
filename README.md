# Dreess-Code 👗
A gift for my mom, with which she can manage and grow her business more ✨

# What is the reason for gifting this to my mom 
Well, I have seen her start and manage a business for 6 years now. She used to struggle with sending pictures personally to every customer on WhatsApp, and there was no proper tracking of the finances, or there was no proper inventory management as this is a small business she has to do everything independently.
Seeing her struggle, I built this web page with AI-assisted development while learning full-stack web technologies—to give her a shareable link where customers can explore her collection anytime.

# Problems I solved 
- A website she shared with her friends; on the first day the website went online, one customer purchased an item using the website.
- Direct WhatsApp ordering, where someone can inquire or place an order directly.
- Connected all her socials in one place.
